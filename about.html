Our mission

Simple tools.
Serious results.

We believe the best tools get out of your way. AllPDFStuff gives you a full suite of professional PDF capabilities — compress, merge, split, convert, extract, watermark, and more — without the friction of desktop software or enterprise subscriptions.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is stored longer than it needs to be. Your files are automatically deleted after one hour. That's not a marketing claim — it's how the platform is built.

Privacy first

Your files are
yours alone.

We take file privacy seriously. Uploaded documents are processed securely, never shared with third parties, and automatically purged from our servers within 60 minutes of processing. We don't train models on your data or analyze your content.

For users who need guaranteed zero retention, our Pro plan supports direct browser-side processing for select tools — meaning your file never leaves your device at all.
