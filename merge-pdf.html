FAQ

Common questions

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

Drop as many files as you like in a single batch — they'll be combined in the order you added them.

Will merging change the formatting of my pages?

No — each page keeps its original layout, size, and orientation; merging only stitches the documents together.

Can I merge Word documents too?

This tool merges PDFs specifically. Convert Word files to PDF first using our Word to PDF tool, then merge.

Is merging PDFs free?

Yes, Merge is one of our free tools — no credit card required to get started.
